# Your Rice — credits

Everything in this rice is original work made for Ricing Manager for Windows, drawn from code:
the cursors come out of `rices/art/RiceArt.cpp` in the
project's repository (Direct2D, DirectWrite and Media Foundation; nothing downloaded and nothing
made by AI).

No picture of ours: the wallpaper you choose is yours, copied into your copy of the rice, and stays on your PC.
