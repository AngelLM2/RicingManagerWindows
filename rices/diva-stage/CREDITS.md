# Diva Stage — credits

Everything in this rice is original work made for Ricing Manager for Windows, drawn from code:
the pictures, the video loop and the cursors come out of `rices/art/RiceArt.cpp` in the
project's repository (Direct2D, DirectWrite and Media Foundation; nothing downloaded and nothing
made by AI).

The video loop (assets/diva-stage.mp4) is drawn frame by frame by the same code.
