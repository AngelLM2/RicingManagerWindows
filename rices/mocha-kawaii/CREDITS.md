# Mocha Kawaii — credits

Everything in this rice is original work made for Ricing Manager for Windows, drawn from code:
the picture and the cursors come out of `rices/art/RiceArt.cpp` in the
project's repository (Direct2D, DirectWrite and Media Foundation; nothing downloaded and nothing
made by AI).

The kaomoji are drawn with fonts that come with Windows. Colors: the Catppuccin Mocha palette (MIT, Catppuccin).
